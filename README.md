# ✈️ Strategic Airline Market Analysis: Pricing, Volume and Dominance using SQL

## 📌 Project Description

This project performs a strategic analysis of the Indian airline industry using SQL.

The objective is to analyze airline pricing strategies, revenue patterns, passenger demand, route dominance, and competitive positioning between premium and low-cost carriers.

By analyzing 15,000+ flight records, this project uncovers how airlines position themselves in the market and how travelers can optimize booking decisions through smarter timing and route selection.

The insights generated support better pricing strategies, optimized scheduling, and revenue-focused decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Problem

The airline industry operates in a highly competitive and price-sensitive environment. Airlines must:

- Optimize ticket pricing  
- Balance premium vs budget positioning  
- Identify high-performing routes  
- Maximize revenue without losing demand  
- Compete effectively across peak and off-peak slots  

This project analyzes airline data to understand pricing dominance, revenue gaps, route performance, and market positioning strategies.

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview

- Analyzed **15,000+ flight records**
- Key attributes include:
  - Airline Name  
  - Source & Destination  
  - Departure Time  
  - Duration

## 🛠️ SQL Techniques Used

- INNER JOIN & LEFT JOIN  
- GROUP BY & Aggregate Functions  
- Subqueries  
- Window Functions (RANK, DENSE_RANK, SUM OVER)  
- CASE Statements  
- Filtering & Conditional Logic  

These techniques were used to evaluate airline-level performance, route dominance, pricing behavior, and time-based fare variations.

## 📊 Key Insights

### Premium Pricing Strategy

- Vistara generated ₹3.89B in revenue — highest average ticket price.
- Air India followed with ₹1.90B revenue.
- Premium carriers dominate through value-based pricing focused on comfort and business travelers.

### Revenue Gaps

- Premium carriers operate in billions in revenue.
- Low-cost carriers operate primarily in millions.
- Budget airlines compete on volume and affordability.

### Route Dynamics

- Metro-to-Metro routes such as Delhi–Mumbai, Bangalore–Delhi, and Mumbai–Bangalore dominate Indian aviation.
- These routes significantly contribute to airline profitability.

### Speed vs Service

- Low-cost carriers (SpiceJet, Go First) often offer faster travel times due to direct routes.
- Premium airlines may have slightly longer durations due to hub-based operations.

### Time-Based Pricing

- Morning and evening flights have the highest fares.
- Afternoon and late-night flights are the cheapest.
- Identified up to 40% fare variation between peak and off-peak travel slots.
